Good Questions, Plain Answers

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?

Spring and fall Saturdays go first, often 8-12 months out. Off-season and weekday dates offer more flexibility and friendlier rates.

Can we bring our own caterer, or is there a preferred list?

The venue works with licensed caterers, and the team will walk you through the options and the prep space when you tour.

Do you provide tables, chairs and set-up?

Yes. Tables and chairs are included with every booking, and the floor plan is laid out with you before event day.

What about music, microphones and slideshows?

House sound covers toasts, background music and presentations. Larger productions can bring in outside A/V or ask about full production partners.

Is there a deposit, and when is the balance due?

A signed agreement and deposit reserve your date. The team explains the payment schedule clearly — no surprises.
